Epilogue · Reading Still Unnamed

Look one up

On Still Unnamed, type a symbol into the lookup and see when it was given and when, if ever, it was renamed. The recent renames there are mostly genes from the 2000s, finally named.

Data: the HGNC complete set as Still Unnamed ships it, as of . A gene’s year is the year HGNC approved it. HGNC dates only a gene’s last rename, so the two chapters use only the genes whose every earlier symbol was a placeholder, for which that date is the day the placeholder went; the with a real name somewhere in their past (ABRAXAS1 was CCDC98 and then FAM175A) are left out of them. Genes HGNC withdrew or merged are not in the file at all. This counts when genes were named, not why. The program is in the repository.
